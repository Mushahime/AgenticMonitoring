By major player

their single biggest recent move, not a minor collab, only shown when we found one worth flagging
Some of these fall outside the two-week window above. Where nothing new happened this fortnight, we kept each player's most recent genuinely significant move rather than stretching for a minor mention.
OWASP · Jun 1, 2026 OWASP GenAI Security Project →

Published "State of Agentic AI Security and Governance" v2.01, a year-on update to its Jul 2025 first edition that reframes agentic AI risk around a year of field evidence rather than speculation, alongside its Dec 2025 OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications (goal hijacking, tool misuse, identity/privilege abuse, supply-chain compromise) as the reference threat model.

Google · May 19, 2026 blog.google →

Launched Universal Cart at I/O 2026, alongside the AP2 update enabling autonomous "human not present" purchases, its biggest single agentic-shopping move to date.

OpenAI · Mar 24, 2026 Digital Commerce 360 →

Ended Instant Checkout after near-zero conversion (Walmart measured it converting roughly 3x worse than clicking through to walmart.com), five months after its Sep 2025 launch. OpenAI now focuses ChatGPT on discovery via ACP and lets merchants run their own checkout in retailer-built apps.

Stripe · Dec 11, 2025 stripe.com →

Launched the Agentic Commerce Suite, its own product rather than a single-partner collaboration, since expanded with Meta and Google partnerships at Stripe Sessions 2026.

Visa · Aug 3, 2026 Visa Investor Relations →

Agreed to acquire behavioral-biometrics fraud firm BioCatch for $2.4B from Permira, bigger than June's Agent Score/Agentic Registry/Large Transaction Model platform launch - a bet that fraud infrastructure becomes the gating issue as AI-powered account takeovers and scams scale alongside agent-initiated transactions.

Mastercard · Aug 3, 2026 Mastercard newsroom →

Completed its $1.8B acquisition of BVNK, five months ahead of schedule - the first major card network to own stablecoin infrastructure outright (BVNK processes roughly $30B in annualized volume across ~200 markets) rather than just partner for it, a bigger structural move than June's Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M) settlement-layer launch.

American Express · Apr 14, 2026 American Express →

Launched the Agentic Commerce Experiences (ACE) developer kit and Agent Purchase Protection, extending purchase backing to card member purchases made by registered AI agents.

Crypto bloc (Coinbase, Binance) · Jul 13-14, 2026 TFTC →

Coinbase's x402 moved to independent foundation governance on Jul 14; a day earlier, Binance upgraded its Agentic Wallet to support x402 payments across BNB Chain, Ethereum, Solana and Base, widening the standard beyond its Base/Coinbase origin.

China bloc (Alipay, Alibaba) · Jul 2, 2026 South China Morning Post →

Ant Group's "Abao" conversational AI assistant, which replaces Alipay's icon-grid home screen with an LLM chat window handling over 10,000 services, moved from invite-only beta (Jun 16) to open public testing on iOS and Android - a bigger structural bet than the May 26 AI Wallet/Token Pay disclosure, built on the same Agentic Commerce Trust Protocol Alipay developed with Alibaba's Qwen app and Taobao Instant Commerce.

Standards bodies (FIDO, EMVCo) · Apr-May 2026 FIDO Alliance →

FIDO Alliance took governance of AP2 (donated by Google, Apr 28) and Verifiable Intent (Mastercard/Google), forming working groups on verifiable instructions, agent authentication and delegation. EMVCo separately confirmed it's evaluating how 3-D Secure, tokenisation and Secure Remote Commerce specs can support agentic payments.

PayPal · Aug 3, 2026 Cryptonomist →

CEO Enrique Lores announced a restructuring that groups PYUSD, Braintree and merchant processing into one dedicated Payment Services & Crypto division, signaling more merchant offerings powered by PYUSD and agentic payments to come - a bigger structural signal than Agent Ready's earlier rollout to PayPal's merchant base (built on the open-source PayPal Agent Toolkit, the PYUSD stablecoin and AP2 participation), disclosed alongside PYUSD's circulating supply falling to roughly $2.7B from over $4B in March.

Amazon · May 27, 2026 GeekWire →

Retired Rufus into Alexa for Shopping, and packaged the underlying tech as the Agentic Shopping Assistant so outside retailers can deploy it in about 60 days - Tapestry's Kate Spade Gift Concierge is the first outside production use.
