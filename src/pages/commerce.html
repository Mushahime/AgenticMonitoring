Ninth Circuit overturns Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's Comet shopping agent

First federal appeals ruling on whether an AI agent "accesses" a website on a user's behalf: the court found Amazon failed to show a Computer Fraud and Abuse Act violation because it's the user, not Perplexity, who directs Comet to Amazon's site (No. 26-1444), vacating the March 2026 San Francisco injunction. A foundational precedent for whether shopping agents can act on retailer sites without permission.
