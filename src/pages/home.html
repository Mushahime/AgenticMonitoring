Top of the stack

10 most consequential signals, mostly from the last week
StablecoinsAug 7, 2026

OSL Group launches OSL AgentPay, multi-stablecoin payment infrastructure for AI agents

Hong Kong-listed, licensed digital-asset platform OSL Group (HKEX: 863) launched AgentPay, letting a developer's AI agent express payment intent - amount, asset, payee - and have OSL handle routing, signing and settlement automatically. Supports USDT, USDC and OSL's own enterprise stablecoin USDGO at launch, across the x402, AP2 and MPP protocols, with zero gas fees and API integration live from day one.

GlobeNewswire
StablecoinsAug 6, 2026

JPYC raises $38M Series B extension, earmarked for AI-agent machine-to-machine payments

Japan's yen-pegged stablecoin issuer closed a ¥6B ($38M) Series B extension led by new investor AZ-COM Maruwa Holdings, a major Japanese logistics firm that plans to pay roughly 2,300 business partners and contractors in JPYC - the first large-scale corporate use of a stablecoin for daily operations in Japan. Funds are earmarked to build out machine-to-machine payment use cases for AI agents and expand JPYC to Ethereum and Polygon.

CoinDesk
NetworkAug 5, 2026

Visa moves stablecoin settlement into production on Visa Direct via Zero Hash

Visa activated live, production-grade stablecoin settlement across Visa Direct - its real-time push-payments rail reaching more than 18 billion endpoints in 195 countries - through an integration with Zero Hash, letting eligible clients pre-fund accounts and disburse cross-border payouts in USDC. Builds directly on July's Visa Stablecoin Platform beta, moving the same initiative from pilot to live infrastructure.

Zero Hash
StablecoinsAug 5, 2026

Circle discloses first Agent Stack adoption numbers in its Q2 2026 results

USDC circulation reached $73.3B (+19% YoY) and on-chain USDC volume hit $14.8T in the quarter (+151% YoY). Circle's Agent Stack, launched in May 2026, now hosts 900+ paid services, with 99.3% of x402 agent-payment volume settling in USDC - the first hard adoption numbers from the largest USD stablecoin issuer's agent-payments product.

Circle
StablecoinsAug 4, 2026

Cloudflare launches Cloudflare Wallets and cloudflare.pay for AI agents

Gives any AI agent deployed on Cloudflare a verifiable identity and a programmable stablecoin wallet built on x402, with spending caps, merchant allowlists and per-transaction limits set by the human owner - built with input from Visa, Mastercard and Amex on the underlying trust standards.

Cloudflare
LegalAug 4, 2026

Ninth Circuit overturns Amazon's injunction against Perplexity's Comet shopping agent

First federal appeals ruling on whether an AI agent "accesses" a website on a user's behalf: the court found it's the user, not Perplexity, who directs Comet to Amazon's site, so no CFAA violation - vacating the March 2026 injunction. A foundational precedent for whether shopping agents can act on retailer sites without permission.

PYMNTS
RegulatoryAug 4, 2026

Senate Judiciary holds first hearing naming AI shopping agents as a surveillance-pricing blind spot

"Your Data, Their Profit: The Consumer Cost of AI Surveillance Pricing" - bipartisan testimony before the Judiciary Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism found no existing federal bill covers AI shopping agents that could exploit real-time behavioral signals on pricing, even as Maryland's and Connecticut's state surveillance-pricing bans take effect Oct 1, 2026.

U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee
FraudAug 3, 2026

Visa agrees to acquire fraud-intelligence firm BioCatch for $2.4B

BioCatch's behavioral-biometrics platform protects 760 million users across more than 350 banking clients by analyzing how people type, swipe and hold their devices - Visa is buying it from Permira as AI-powered account-takeover and scam attempts scale alongside agent-initiated transactions.

Visa Investor Relations
StablecoinsAug 3, 2026

Mastercard completes $1.8B acquisition of BVNK, owning stablecoin rails outright

Deal closed five months ahead of schedule, making Mastercard the first major card network to own (not just partner for) stablecoin infrastructure - BVNK processes roughly $30B in annualized stablecoin volume across some 200 markets, relevant as machine-to-machine agent payments increasingly lean on stablecoin settlement.

Mastercard newsroom
FundingJul 21, 2026

Ant International raises ~$1.2B Series A, among the largest rounds yet in agentic-commerce infrastructure

Backed by existing shareholders Ant Group and Alibaba plus new international investors, valuing the cross-border payments firm near $10B. Ant International connects more than 150 million merchants and 2 billion user accounts across its Alipay+, Antom, WorldFirst and Bettr businesses; the raise funds AI-powered merchant payment, treasury and credit tools and its agentic-commerce push - AI systems handling product discovery, purchasing and payment end to end - positioning it directly against Stripe and PayPal for the agentic settlement layer.
