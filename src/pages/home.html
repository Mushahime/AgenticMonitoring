FundingAug 13, 2026

Singapore's Graas raises $17M Series B and buys Trustana to feed its Commerce Knowledge Graph

Round led by LemmaTree, founded by Temasek, with Integra Partners and Tin Men Capital. Graas simultaneously acquired product-data platform Trustana, folding its enrichment layer into the Commerce Knowledge Graph that underpins the AI agents Graas deploys for retail clients, and opening Australia and the Middle East. Its B2B agentic business is growing over 100% year on year.

CryptoAug 12, 2026

x402 settlement volume down 93% year to date, a reality check on the agent economy narrative

Analyst Jamie Coutts, citing Helios Analytics data from Oct 2025 to Jul 2026, puts x402's seven-day average settlement volume near $41,800, down 93% YTD and 55% over three months - evidence the late-2025 surge was largely testing, not sustained demand. He pairs it with Cloudflare's Monetization Gateway, live since Jul 1, as proof infrastructure keeps expanding while volume stays depressed.

CryptoPotato
Standards bodyAug 12, 2026

Solv Labs ships verifiable, auditable agent payments on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

AWS published the reference build: each agent transaction is authorized by Solv's ORACLE policy engine, attested per-transaction inside an AWS Nitro Enclave, priced for risk and anchored to a public blockchain before settling over x402. A shipped answer to the question FACT and ATLAS frame - how an agent payment carries portable, tamper-resistant evidence of what was authorized - rather than another proposal.

PlatformAug 11, 2026

Shoplazza's Athena agent adds millisecond payment routing to conversational store operations

At its DTC Summit in Shenzhen, Shoplazza detailed the current build of Athena, the AI commerce agent it launched on Product Hunt on Jul 26. Athena now dynamically selects the optimal payment routing path for each transaction in milliseconds, weighing card type, issuing bank and transaction timing, alongside Smart Express Checkout upgrades in Shoplazza Payments aimed at cutting checkout failures. A merchant-side agent that owns payment routing, not just the storefront conversation.

StablecoinsAug 7, 2026

OSL Group launches OSL AgentPay, multi-stablecoin payment infrastructure for AI agents

Hong Kong-listed, licensed digital-asset platform OSL Group (HKEX: 863) launched AgentPay, letting a developer's AI agent express payment intent - amount, asset, payee - and have OSL handle routing, signing and settlement automatically. Supports USDT, USDC and OSL's own enterprise stablecoin USDGO at launch, across the x402, AP2 and MPP protocols, with zero gas fees and API integration live from day one.

StablecoinsAug 6, 2026

JPYC raises $38M Series B extension, earmarked for AI-agent machine-to-machine payments

Japan's yen-pegged stablecoin issuer closed a ¥6B ($38M) Series B extension led by new investor AZ-COM Maruwa Holdings, a major Japanese logistics firm that plans to pay roughly 2,300 business partners and contractors in JPYC - the first large-scale corporate use of a stablecoin for daily operations in Japan. Funds are earmarked to build out machine-to-machine payment use cases for AI agents and expand JPYC to Ethereum and Polygon.

NetworkAug 5, 2026

Visa moves stablecoin settlement into production on Visa Direct via Zero Hash

Visa activated live, production-grade stablecoin settlement across Visa Direct - its real-time push-payments rail reaching more than 18 billion endpoints in 195 countries - through an integration with Zero Hash, letting eligible clients pre-fund accounts and disburse cross-border payouts in USDC. Builds directly on July's Visa Stablecoin Platform beta, moving the same initiative from pilot to live infrastructure.

Circle discloses first Agent Stack adoption numbers in its Q2 2026 results

USDC circulation reached $73.3B (+19% YoY) and on-chain USDC volume hit $14.8T in the quarter (+151% YoY). Circle's Agent Stack, launched in May 2026, now hosts 900+ paid services, with 99.3% of x402 agent-payment volume settling in USDC - the first hard adoption numbers from the largest USD stablecoin issuer's agent-payments product.

Yellow Card raises $40M from Standard Chartered's SC Ventures and Sony Innovation Fund

Strategic round with SC Ventures, Sony Innovation Fund, Polychain Capital and Blockchain Capital takes total equity past $120M, funding Global USD Accounts and the stablecoin rails connecting it, targeted at Latin America and Asia Pacific. Notable less for the size than for who wrote the cheque: a global bank's venture arm and a Japanese conglomerate, not the crypto-native funds behind every prior round.

FundingAug 4, 2026

Zenity raises $125M Series C to police what autonomous agents actually do at runtime

Led by Norwest with SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Hitachi Ventures, LG Technology Ventures and Intel Capital, taking total funding to roughly $185M. The pitch is deliberately not model protection or prompt filtering: it governs the runtime actions of agents already inside enterprise environments - which tools they invoke, which data they touch, which workflows they execute. The same authorization-and-evidence problem FACT and ATLAS track, funded at scale on the enterprise security side.
