FundingAug 13, 2026

Singapore's Graas raises $17M Series B and buys Trustana to feed its Commerce Knowledge Graph

Series B led by LemmaTree, an investment firm founded by Temasek, with Integra Partners and Tin Men Capital. Graas simultaneously acquired Trustana, a Singapore product-data platform that automates enrichment and structuring of product information, folding it into the Commerce Knowledge Graph that underpins the AI agents Graas deploys for retail clients - the stated aim being agents that recommend and sell across text, voice and vision. The acquisition also opens Australia and the Middle East. Graas reports revenue growing over 50% year on year and its B2B agentic business over 100%; integration is due by end-December.

FundingAug 4, 2026

Zenity raises $125M Series C to police what autonomous agents actually do at runtime

Series C led by Norwest with SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Hitachi Ventures, LG Technology Ventures, Qumra Capital, Vertex Ventures, Third Point Ventures, DTCP and Intel Capital, taking total funding to roughly $185M. Zenity's pitch is deliberately not model protection or prompt filtering: it governs the runtime actions of agents already inside enterprise environments - which tools they invoke, which data they touch, which workflows they execute. The same authorization-and-evidence problem the FACT and ATLAS pages track, funded at scale on the enterprise security side rather than the payments side.

RegulatoryAug 4, 2026

Senate Judiciary holds first hearing naming AI shopping agents as a surveillance-pricing blind spot

"Your Data, Their Profit: The Consumer Cost of AI Surveillance Pricing" - bipartisan testimony before the Judiciary Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism, chaired by Sen. Josh Hawley, found no existing federal bill covers AI shopping agents that could exploit real-time behavioral or emotional signals on pricing. Four states have already enacted surveillance-pricing bans, with Maryland's and Connecticut's taking effect Oct 1, 2026.

Bengaluru's Kily raises ₹30 crore (~$3.1M) seed round

Led by Sorin Investments with Razorpay and Wyser Capital participating - Kily's AI agents automate product visibility, pricing, advertising and inventory decisions for consumer brands across Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, with funds going toward product development and India go-to-market expansion. FundingAug 3, 2026

Maximum emerges from stealth with $30M to build an AI-native bank core

One of the largest seed rounds in fintech, led by CRV with Pear VC, Anthemis and others - ex-Marqeta exec Randy Fernando's startup builds an AI-native operating system letting banks deploy custom agents on top of, and eventually replacing, legacy cores. More than 70% of the roughly 5,000 US banks still run on infrastructure built before mobile banking, real-time payments or AI existed.

AdoptionJul 31, 2026

Mastercard puts agentic commerce at the centre of its Q2 2026 earnings call

CEO Michael Miebach framed Agent Pay as a major opportunity as commerce moves to AI-driven transactions, and argued card networks stay central because merchants and consumers still require reach, security and predictable payment experiences. Management confirmed machine-to-machine capabilities in development for AI agent transactions, noting these may need alternative settlement methods such as stablecoins. Reported alongside net revenues up 12% currency-neutral and switching penetration at 72%. A read on how a network frames the agentic thesis to investors rather than in a product launch.

FundingJul 29, 2026

Freehand raises $75M Series B to put AI agents in charge of Fortune 500 supply-chain spend

Co-led by Battery Ventures and NewRoad Capital Partners, with Penny Pritzker's PSP Growth and Nexus Venture Partners participating, bringing total funding to $100M. Freehand's AI agents operate inside customers' existing systems - reading contracts, invoices and policies - to verify bills, track milestones and negotiate vendor contracts for non-standard supply-chain spend (logistics, raw materials, parts, labor); serves roughly 50 customers including Meta, Unilever, Johnson & Johnson, Pfizer and Cardinal Health, autonomously processing billions of dollars in payments across 60-70 countries.

Ant International raises ~$1.2B Series A, among the largest rounds yet in agentic-commerce infrastructure

Backed by existing shareholders Ant Group and Alibaba plus new international investors, valuing the cross-border payments firm near $10B. Ant International connects more than 150 million merchants and 2 billion user accounts across its Alipay+, Antom, WorldFirst and Bettr businesses; the raise funds AI-powered merchant payment, treasury and credit tools and its agentic-commerce push - AI systems handling product discovery, purchasing and payment end to end - positioning it directly against Stripe and PayPal for the agentic settlement layer.
