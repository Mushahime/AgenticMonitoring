Hashed Open Research: a won stablecoin needs its own settlement rails and digital ID for the agent economy

Policy arm of Korean VC firm Hashed published "Strategies for Won Stablecoins in the Age of the Agent Economy," arguing that with roughly 98.6% of current AI-agent stablecoin payments denominated in dollar stablecoins, a competitive won-backed stablecoin needs its own settlement network and digital-identity infrastructure rather than just an issuance license - citing Stripe and Circle's vertical integration of issuance and settlement (Circle's Arc) as the competitive benchmark South Korea's stablecoin push has to match.
